Customers & booked hours

Calendar lists months in chronological order (oldest first, through month last). After load, the view scrolls to the bottom so the through month is in view—scroll up for earlier months. Each month has a stats panel on the left: totals, bar comparison to the prior one–two months in your loaded range, and simple daily trend lines (day 1 → end of month). Comparisons use the same day-of-month window when the month is still in progress. Set how many months back (load at least 2–3 to compare trends). List tab uses the same date limits (max about 800 days / two years). Customers = headcount: couple = 2, single = 1. Cancelled / no-show excluded. 💻 / 📞 = appointment counts booked online (customer) vs by staff (Square merchant/team); appointments with an unknown booker are not counted in either. Days before yesterday use stored totals after first load; use Refresh past from Square to rebuild (large ranges can be slow).
